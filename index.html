Portal Calculations Guide:
  • Quizzes (15%) & Assignments (10%): Dynamically aggregated using Sum(Obtained) / Sum(Total) * Weight.
  • Midterm (25%): Typically graded out of 25. Terminal (50%): Typically graded out of 50.
  • Lab Component: Lab Assignments are weighted at 25%, Lab Midterm at 25%, and Lab Terminal/Project at 50%.
  • Critical Passing Rule: Both Theory and Lab components MUST separately aggregate ≥ 50% to pass. Otherwise, the course receives an F grade (0.0 GP).
